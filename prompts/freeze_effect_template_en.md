# 🎁 FREEZE EFFECT VIDEO PROMPT (template original)

> Modelo de referência usado para escrever `prompts/C.txt` (o clipe do congelamento).
> Mantenha o esqueleto ([SCENE] → [TIME FREEZE] → [FROZEN CAMERA] → [RESUME] → [CONTINUITY] → [RULES]) e troque o conteúdo pela sua cena.

## Image 1 — EXACT Reference FRAME

Use Image 1 as the exact reference frame of the video, not merely as a character reference. Begin the video directly from this image without changing its composition, camera angle, framing, character position, scale, appearance, clothing, pose, objects, lighting, colors, background, or depth of field. Preserve every visible element exactly at the start, then continue the action naturally from this established frame.

## [SCENE]

Daytime on a wide Manhattan sidewalk. New York atmosphere: pedestrians, storefronts, brick façades, traffic lights, yellow taxis, cyclists, reflections, and street steam.

The man matches Image 1. He wears a plain white oversized T-shirt, dark trousers, and black sneakers. No chain, jewelry, or accessories.

He carries an overfilled sketchbook, a bright red folder, loose illustrated pages, printed photographs, colorful sticky notes, several markers, a phone, and a transparent cup of iced coffee. Everything is stacked precariously in his arms, creating the feeling that one small impact could send paper, photographs, pens, ice cubes, and coffee flying in every direction.

A young dark-haired woman approaches from the opposite direction in a neutral jacket, jeans, sneakers, and headphones, looking at her phone.

## [STYLE]

Ultra-realistic cinematic live-action. Natural skin and fabric, accurate motion, shallow depth of field, daylight bloom, handheld 35mm look.

## [CAMERA]

One continuous tracking shot follows behind and beside the man with natural handheld motion.

## [COLLISION]

The man turns a building corner while adjusting the stack. The woman enters his path and they collide shoulder-to-shoulder.

The sketchbook flips open, the red folder releases pages, photographs and illustrations rotate, sticky notes scatter, markers spin, the phone drops, and the cup tilts as coffee, droplets, and ice cubes fly outward with realistic physics.

The man falls backward away from her. She steps forward and extends her arm toward him.

## [TIME FREEZE]

At the midpoint of his fall, time stops completely. Pedestrians freeze mid-step, vehicles stop, while hair, clothes, steam, all belongings, coffee droplets, and ice cubes hang motionless.

The woman stands directly in front of his fall path, arm extended, hand aligned with his wrist/forearm. She is not behind him, touching him, or pulling him. Every object remains fixed.

## [FROZEN CAMERA]

The camera moves between suspended pages, photographs, markers, ice cubes, and coffee droplets. Close-ups show paper texture, drawings, condensation, and reflections.

It orbits the man, showing his backward-fall posture, expression, and identity, then reveals the woman's hand at the future interception point. She catches him only after time resumes.

## [RESUME]

The camera frames both characters. Time resumes instantly without an effect. The woman catches his wrist/forearm at the established point, absorbs his momentum, stabilizes him, and safely returns him to balance without aggressive pulling.

His belongings and coffee continue from their frozen positions and scatter across the sidewalk. The street resumes normally. He looks at her in surprise, then at the items. She releases him once stable. They briefly face each other as New York life continues.

## [CONTINUITY]

The man remains identical to Image 1 in the same white oversized T-shirt with no jewelry. All objects come from his belongings. Physics and positions remain consistent. The woman stays in front of his fall path. Nothing appears, disappears, duplicates, or teleports.

## [RULES]

One continuous shot. No cuts, transitions, slow motion, teleportation, or stylized effects. The freeze is environmental stillness while the camera moves. Realistic anatomy and physics.

## One more tip

Don't overload the freeze with random objects just because they look cool.

Give every suspended element a reason to be there.

The papers are flying because the folder opened.

The coffee is suspended because the cup tilted.

The character is frozen because he was already falling.

The stronger the cause-and-effect logic is before the freeze, the more believable the impossible moment becomes.
